01

Warehousing & Inventory Management

Our warehousing solutions are designed to help you manage inventory efficiently and securely.

  • Ambient & Temperature-Controlled Storage: Specialized environments for everything from room temperature to ultra-cold storage.
  • Dangerous Goods (DG) Storage: Compliant, secure storage for hazardous materials, adhering to safety regulations.
  • Medical & Pharma Storage: Ensuring the integrity of sensitive goods with climate-controlled storage that meets pharmaceutical standards.
  • Advanced Racking Systems: Maximize space utilization with cutting-edge racking solutions.
  • Real-Time Inventory Tracking: Seamlessly track your stock in real-time with our advanced Warehouse Management System (WMS).
  • Scalable Storage Solutions: Flexible storage designed to grow with your business across various sectors (FMCG, retail, industrial, etc.).
02

Distribution & Logistics

Our logistics services ensure the safe, timely, and efficient delivery of your products.

  • Last-Mile Delivery: We provide fast, reliable last-mile solutions designed to exceed customer expectations.
  • Cross-Docking & Transshipment: Minimize storage time with efficient goods transfer, speeding up your supply chain.
  • Route Optimization & Fleet Management: Our fleet management solutions ensure cost-effective deliveries and maximize fleet utilization.
03

Value-Added Services (VAS)

We enhance the efficiency of your supply chain with additional services that add value at every stage.

  • E-Commerce Fulfillment & Marketplace Integration: Seamless integration with platforms like Shopify, ensuring efficient order fulfillment and returns management.
  • Packaging, Labeling & Kitting: Custom packaging solutions that ensure your products are retail-ready and compliant with industry standards.
  • Assembly & Quality Inspection: We provide assembly and quality control to ensure your products are market-ready.
  • Returns Management & Refurbishing: Comprehensive reverse logistics services, including returns processing, refurbishing, and recycling.
04

Third-Party Logistics (3PL)

End-to-end logistics solutions that integrate with your supply chain, providing a complete outsourced 3PL service.

  • End-to-End Supply Chain Management: From procurement to final delivery, we manage every step of your logistics needs, offering seamless service.
  • Customs Clearance & Trade Compliance: Expertly handling import/export documentation and ensuring smooth cross-border movement.
  • Project Cargo Handling & Specialized Services: Safe and efficient transportation of oversized, heavy, or industrial cargo.
05

Freight Forwarding & International Logistics

Expand your reach globally with our comprehensive freight forwarding solutions.

  • Multimodal Freight Forwarding: Whether by air, sea, road, or rail, we provide efficient, reliable solutions for your international logistics.
  • International Customs Brokerage: We manage import/export documentation, ensuring full compliance with international trade regulations.
